    Class FrameClock

    Ticks once per display refresh on the UI thread, at the screen's real rate (60, 120 Hz or more), for smooth animation. Uses CADisplayLink on iOS and Mac Catalyst and Choreographer on Android; elsewhere a timer at most MaximumRate times per second.

    Remarks

    iPhones run apps at 60 Hz unless the app's Info.plist sets CADisableMinimumFrameDurationOnPhone to true. Stop the clock while nothing animates: a running clock keeps the display at its highest rate.
