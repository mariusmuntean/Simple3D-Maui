    Showcases

    The MAUI gallery uses fifteen shared scene factories. Each demonstrates a different use:

    Scene Capability Gallery animation Image
    Equipment Named grouped primitives, lines, arrows, labels Output vector sweeps Equipment
    Packing Orthographic camera, repeated packages Packages lift and settle Packing
    Surface Procedural indexed mesh and depth picking Sample marker follows the wave Surface
    Assembly Pickable parts of a camera module Front element travels to focus Assembly
    Molecule Scientific model made from spheres and bonds Whole molecule rotates Molecule
    Telemetry Orthographic chart with named samples Bars change height and color within 0–2.5 Telemetry
    City Architectural massing with selectable blocks Building masses rise and settle City
    Robot Arm Nested transforms and individually pickable joints Shoulder and elbow articulate Robot Arm
    Orbit Parent-child motion and an orbital path Planet revolves while its moon follows Orbit
    Wind Three blades sharing a rotating hub Turbine rotor spins while its tower stays fixed Wind
    Conveyor Inspection Visual inspection and status on a production line Parcels cross a fixed scanner and change color after passing Conveyor Inspection
    Solar Tracker Energy equipment with a moving sun marker A panel tilts to face the marker above its fixed mount Solar Tracker
    Packet Routing Selectable network devices, links and traffic Packets traverse two links through a router Packet Routing
    Drone Survey A field survey vehicle with individually pickable parts The drone hovers as four propellers spin over its pad Drone Survey
    Patterned Surface Checker cells made from colored meshes with fixed directional lighting Surface tilts to show shading; each cell stays selectable Patterned Surface

    Render one scene or all fifteen:

    dotnet run --project tests/Simple3D.Core.Tests -c Release -- --render-images Equipment equipment.ppm
dotnet run --project tests/Simple3D.Core.Tests -c Release -- --render-images --all output

    The Core runner exports the initial still frame; --render-images skips its tests. Open PPM files in an image viewer or convert them to PNG. Run python3 scripts/render-doc-images.py to regenerate the documentation images. The shared factories demonstrate groups, meshes, materials, camera fitting and time-based animation. The gallery adds selection, orbit and zoom with a 60-updates-per-second animation target. Actual displayed frame rate depends on the device and viewport. The Solar Tracker's sun is a visual marker; lighting uses the renderer's fixed direction.

    Patterned Surface uses separate colored mesh cells, not image texture mapping. Its parts are built once and reused during animation. The current renderer provides flat directional lighting; it does not provide physically based materials or shadows.
